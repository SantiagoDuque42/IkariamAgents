How growth works in this model

1 · Fill the housing you already have

Serve wine at the tavern's full level, put every citizen to work (gold is usually idle early), and turn on any +20% production items. Empty housing fills in days, and it is the cheapest growth there is.

2 · Build by payback

The Plan and Towns tabs rank builds by payback: cost divided by the extra output per hour, plus build time. Museums, Carpenter/Architect levels, Forester and luxury-bonus buildings usually come first.

3 · Expand

Before founding a new town, raise the Palace and every Governor's Residence to the new number of towns, so corruption stays at 0. Turn on "Found a new town" above to simulate it and see when it pays off.

4 · Compound

Town Hall + Tavern pairs as satisfaction allows, cost reducers in the towns that build most (check them with the Calculator), Gods' Shrine levels, and research from citizens the island caps can't employ.

Rules of thumb

  • Marble is often the binding resource: upgrades need about as much marble as wood, and few towns mine it.
  • Population beats taxes while gold is idle.
  • Satisfaction must exceed housing, or the town never fills: a Town Hall level without a matching tavern or museum gain is wasted.
  • Keep a wine buffer of about 36 hours of consumption in every town.
  • Corruption = 1 − (residence level + 1) ÷ number of towns. Raise every residence before founding a town, never after.

Import from the game

The snapshot bookmark reads your towns straight from the game: building levels, population, warehouse stock, wine served, troops and ships, free building grounds, and sawmill and mine levels. It only reads; it changes nothing in your game.

  1. Once: create a new bookmark in your browser, name it "Ikariam snapshot", and paste the code below as its URL (or drag this link to your bookmarks bar: Ikariam snapshot).
  2. Each time: open your game tab, click the bookmark and wait about 10 seconds. It saves a file for the Drive inbox and shows a box: press Copy for planner.
  3. Paste it here and press Import game snapshot. Research, gods, favour and your free-ground plans are kept; everything the game shows is replaced.

Bookmark code

This short bookmark loads the snapshot tool (Mayor 0.3.0) from the IkariamAgents repository through jsDelivr, and the browser checks its fingerprint before running it. Building cost calculator

Exact cost of taking one building from one level to another. Each level costs base × (1 − total reduction %), rounded down; research, reducer buildings and the extra % add up, they don't multiply. Reducer levels are capped at 50.

Reductions

Formulas the planner uses

Housing 2·⌊10·L^1.5⌋ + 40 (Town Hall L ≤ 40) + 50 Holiday + 50 capital (Well Digging) + 200 capital (Utopia) + 20 per Economic Future. Matched real towns exactly when checked.

Satisfaction = 196 + 25 Holiday + 50 capital + tavern-level bonus (12, 24, 36… 236 at 18) + wine-served bonus (60 per step, rising: 1,182 at level 18) + museum (20, 41, 63, 88…) + 50 per cultural good, then × (1 − corruption). Real populations sat within about 20 of these values when checked.

Growth: population moves toward satisfaction with a 50-hour time constant: P(t) = S − (S − P₀)·e^(−t/50), i.e. 0.02 × (S − P) per hour, capped at housing.

Wine consumed per hour at serving level n (0, 4, 8, 13, 18 … 150 at 18), × (1 − Wine Press level %).

Production: 1 per worker per hour × (1 + 2% per Forester/luxury-bonus level + 20% boost + god blessing). Helping Hands adds 50% more workers at ¼ output. Worker caps by island level (sawmill 34 = 858, mine 33 = 1,079).

Gold: 3 per taxed citizen per hour; a scientist costs 6 (3 with Letter Chute) and produces 1.14 research points with Paper, Ink and Mechanical Pen.

Costs: base cost × (1 − 14% research − reducer level %), reducers capped at level 50. Base costs for every level come from the in-game help tables.

Gods' Shrine: maximum blessing 3 + 2 × level %; without Favour a blessed town gets 40% of it, with 1,000 Favour the full amount.

Corruption: 1 − (Governor's Residence level + 1) ÷ towns; none in the capital while Palace ≥ number of colonies.

Assumptions to confirm

  • Confirmed in the game (Nov 2026): Town Hall 19→20 with Carpenter 12, Architect 14 and all three cost researches costs 28,817 wood and 29,458 marble, exactly as modelled.
  • Free building grounds and island levels come from the snapshot or from what you type in Update state.
  • The Gods' Shrine blesses as many towns as its level, in the order you list.
  • The +20% items apply to all towns.
  • The Premium Trader swaps resources 1:1 in one town per use, up to warehouse capacity.
  • Resources flow freely between your towns (the model pools stock; ships take time in reality).
